Privacy

The app

Loadout runs on your computer. It has no account, sends no analytics and collects nothing about you. It contacts only GitHub: github.com and codeload.github.com to download skills you choose, and GitHub Releases to check for a newer version of Loadout. Everything it keeps stays on your computer, in ~/.loadout and ~/.claude.

This website

The site is static and is served by Cloudflare, which keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and browser) to run and protect the service. The site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics.
